# Sibling update — required content

A separate five-line document, readable in under a minute, pasteable into a
family chat. It is written by its own `save-text` call, not appended to the
brief.

## Required shape

- **Five lines.** No more, no fewer.
- Plain language. No medical terminology beyond the drug names.
- No dosage advice and no instruction to change anything.
- It may say a question is waiting for the doctor; it must not answer it.
- It must not introduce any fact that is not in the brief.

## Required content

1. The appointment happened on 8 November 2026 and the brief is ready.
2. The medication list is current, with the sources.
3. The one open item: the label and the letter disagree about the metformin
   dose, and the doctor needs to settle it.
4. The two questions the family asked, waiting for the doctor.
5. The blurry photo could not be read and is listed as unreadable.

## Example

> Dad's appointment was on 8 November and the brief is ready for the doctor.
> The medication list is up to date, taken from the pill labels and the clinic letter.
> One thing needs the doctor: the metformin label and the letter give different doses.
> We asked about the blood pressure tablet with the antibiotics, and about the statin timing.
> The blurry photo could not be read, so it is listed as unreadable rather than guessed.

The example is a guide to length and tone, not a script. The Skill writes its own
words; the five facts above are what must be there.
